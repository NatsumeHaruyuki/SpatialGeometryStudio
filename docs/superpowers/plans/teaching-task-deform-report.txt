Vertex deformation

src/geometry/deform.ts exports deformSolid(solid, pointId, desired). It returns a new solid with the original topology, identifiers, and metadata. Prism classification checks opposite face geometry and matched cross edges, using the greatest separation of face centers along Y. An upper vertex translates the upper face, and a lower vertex projects onto the lower face and carries its corresponding upper vertex. A quadrangular pyramid uses a planar base and a free apex. A tetrahedron and other polyhedra move the specified vertex freely.

The helper checks vertex IDs, finite coordinates, coincident vertices, and collapsed prism height. The scene parser checks convexity, face validity, and construction dependencies. The rotational-solid helper owns round control points.
